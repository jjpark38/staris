Five Korean Signatures — each is a Tier-locked affix in the loot pool

01 · MAGIC TIER · OFFENSIVE

Saktoo's Edge

5cm 어긋남 · 5cm Misalignment

The Saktoo — a ritual blade walked barefoot by Korean shamans. The 5cm misalignment between left and right foot is the rhythm of imbalance. Off-balance is the signature, not the flaw.

Affix · Magic tier · weapon-class trigger
02 · LEGENDARY · OFFENSIVE

9,860 Won Cadence

9,860원의 박자 · approx. $7 USD

9,860 KRW (~$7 USD) — the price of one Korean meal, the 2024 minimum wage cadence. In-game: damage scales as HP drops. The lower the wage, the harder the swing. Season 2 exclusive.

Season 2 · Legendary (permanent retention, v2.4)
03 · TIER 90 · UTILITY

Veil of Dangun

단군왕검의 건(巾) · founder deity, 2333 BCE

Dangun — founder of the first Korean kingdom (2333 BCE). The veil represents shamanic communion with heaven. Equipping it expands the player's vision and item-pickup range.

Tier 90 · Accessory skin · headband / necklace
04 · TIER 50 · UTILITY

60fps Cadence

60fps 풀스로틀 · the tempo metaphor

A Korean idiom: "normal speed = 60fps." The hero refuses to slow down. Counter to a Mt. Hua sect that down-converts time to 24fps. Grants slow-immunity + attack-speed bonus.

Tier 50 · Magic-Mythic · attack-speed cadence
05 · TIER 60 · DEFENSIVE

Way of Five Directions

오방위의 도(道) · Korean Daoist 5-bearings cosmology

Korean Daoist cosmology — five bearings (East/West/South/North/Center), each color-coded. The hero's counter to area-debuff formations from the Wudang sect's Cheongcheon. Resists 5-target AoE debuffs by up to 22% (Mythic). Pairs with Saktoo's Edge for layered defense.

Tier 60 · Rare to Mythic · area-debuff resistance
